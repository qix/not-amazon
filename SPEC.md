❯ I'm building a local product listing website paroday website of Amazon, calling it
  "Not Amazon.com"

  Create a website based on Next.js, where the index page shows a map based on your
  current direction. A search bar at the top searches for products rather than location
   names. It should show a sidebar of all the products matching your search from the
  stores nearby, along with a cropped photo of that product.

  Any user can list a new store on the website. When you list a store you post a number
   of photos from inside the store. Each photo is automatically scanned for any
  products listed in the store, and then that is added to the database. When saving
  each product, also crop the photo to just that product and add it to the listing.

  Use a local sqlite database for all of the products listed.
